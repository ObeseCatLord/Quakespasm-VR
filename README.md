# Quakespasm VR

Quakespasm VR is a Quakespasm fork on VR play, mod compatibility, and streamlined co-op. 
It continues gameflorist's QuakeSpasm-OpenVR lineage and VR controls, then adds CSQC
compatibility, QSS-M-inspired networking, co-op quality-of-life behavior,
modern particle/weather support, and per-mod VR weapon calibration.

## Features
-Full compatibility with popular mods like Arcane Dimensions, Alkaline, Quake Brutalist Jam 3, Dwell, and all the official campaigns
-Improved VR synced netcode
-A weapon wheel available to both VR and desktop players
-Streamlined co-op options including the ability to respawn and teleport on top of other players
-Per mod weapon offset calibration with popular mods having built in offsets
-Full support for the 2021 re-release models
-Auto-detection of the 2021 re-release
-A mod downloader and browser
